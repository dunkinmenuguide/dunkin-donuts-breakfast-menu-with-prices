Dunkin Donuts breakfast menu with prices

Dunkin' built its empire on coffee and donuts, but the breakfast menu is what keeps the drive-thru line wrapped around the building every morning. From flaky croissant sandwiches to grab-and-go Wake-Up Wraps, the morning lineup is bigger — and better value — than most people expect. One important note before the details: Dunkin' is a franchise system, so the numbers below are typical ranges, not guarantees. A breakfast sandwich that costs one price in Ohio might cost a dollar more in Manhattan or at an airport location. Always treat these as ballparks — prices vary by location, and your local shop's menu board is the final word.

Breakfast sandwiches

The backbone of the morning menu is the egg-and-cheese sandwich, and you build it your way: bacon or sausage, on a croissant, bagel, or English muffin. The croissant version is the fan favorite — buttery, flaky layers wrapped around a folded egg, melted cheese, and your choice of meat. It eats like a proper bakery sandwich, not fast food. The bagel version is chewier and more filling, while the English muffin is the lightest of the three and the easiest to eat one-handed in the car.

A breakfast sandwich typically runs around $4 to $6 depending on the bread and meat you choose, with the croissant usually priced at the top of that range. Doubles — extra meat or extra egg — cost more but turn the sandwich into something that will genuinely hold you until lunch. Pairing any sandwich with a coffee is usually cheaper than buying the two items separately.

Wake-Up Wraps

Wake-Up Wraps are Dunkin's most underrated breakfast item. Each one is a small, warm flour tortilla folded around egg, cheese, and bacon or sausage — basically a breakfast sandwich in miniature. They are the perfect size when you want something warm and savory but not a full meal, and at typically just a couple of dollars each, they are among the cheapest hot items on the menu.

The real pro move is ordering two wraps instead of one sandwich. You get variety, built-in portion control, and often a lower total price. They also travel better than any sandwich — no crumbs, no unwrapping ceremony, just a tidy little package you can eat with one hand.

Hash browns and sides

No breakfast order is complete without hash browns, and Dunkin's version — crispy, seasoned potato rounds — has a devoted following. They come in a six-piece order that is sized as a side rather than a meal, which makes them the ideal add-on to a sandwich or wrap. Expect to pay typically around $2 to $3 for an order, though again, prices vary by location.

Hash browns also play well with the app: they show up regularly in combo deals and limited-time value offers. If you are already ordering a sandwich and coffee, adding hash browns as part of a combo is almost always cheaper than adding them a la carte.

Muffins, bagels and bakery

The bakery case covers the sweeter side of breakfast. Muffins come in rotating flavors — blueberry and chocolate chip are the longtime staples — and they are substantial enough to count as breakfast on their own. Bagels are available plain or as carriers for the breakfast sandwiches, and you can also get them with cream cheese as a standalone order.

Bakery items generally sit in the $2 to $4 range each, making them a solid middle ground between a Munchkins box and a full sandwich. They are also the items most likely to vary by store: some locations bake a wider muffin rotation than others, so the case at your neighborhood shop might hold a surprise or two.

Typical price ranges at a glance

Putting it together, here is how the morning menu usually shakes out. Wake-Up Wraps are the budget pick at typically a couple of dollars each. Hash browns and bakery items land in the $2 to $4 range. Breakfast sandwiches — the main event — typically run around $4 to $6. Add a medium hot coffee at roughly $2.50 to $3.50 and a complete breakfast for one person usually totals somewhere in the $7 to $10 range before tax.

Final thoughts

The Dunkin Donuts breakfast menu with prices boils down to a simple truth: it is one of the best values in fast-food breakfast. Wraps for a light bite, croissant sandwiches when you mean business, hash browns as the essential side — all at prices that undercut most competitors. Order ahead in the app, watch for combo deals, and you will eat well for under ten bucks.
